# T1 and T2: test results

Each table averages saved holdout metrics equally across model seeds. Bold identifies the smallest unrounded mean in the first two columns. Sample standard deviations use n−1 and appear only in the sentence below each table.

OPF solution error = bus MSE + generator MSE, combining voltage-angle errors (rad²) with voltage-magnitude and power errors (p.u.²). Constraint violation = (power-balance L2 + thermal-excess L2) / √(number of buses); thermal excess uses max(|S|² − limit², 0) at both branch ends. It combines p.u. and p.u.², so both columns are composite scores. Here p.u. means per unit; bound residuals are separate diagnostics.

Cost difference is the saved per-sample mean of 100 × (predicted generation cost − reference generation cost) / reference generation cost, then averaged across seeds. The reference is the dataset's AC-OPF solver solution. Results evaluate the validation-selected best.pt; its training count can precede the completed budget.

## Consistency and AC-OPF interpretation

- **T1**: 120 result files and 120 runs passed the consistency checks. Selected checkpoints span 120,000–1,000,000 training presentations. Largest per-seed case violation: 722.9312 (GAT, case500, seed 43). Signed mean cost gaps span -16.453% to +92.136%; 71 of 120 per-case seed results have negative gaps.
- **T2**: 288 result files and 72 runs passed the consistency checks. Selected checkpoints span 360,000–1,000,000 training presentations. Largest per-seed case violation: 317.7879 (GIN, collection {57, 118, 500}, case500, seed 42). Signed mean cost gaps span -25.087% to +32.060%; 116 of 216 per-case seed results have negative gaps.

Checks cover finite/nonnegative errors, metric identities, seed coverage, training budgets, holdout hashes within each case/collection, reference costs across campaigns, and T2 collection aggregation. T2 hashes include collection index offsets and cannot be compared directly with T1 hashes. Large residuals indicate poor constraint satisfaction even when solution MSE is small. A negative cost gap alone does not establish an improvement over the reference solver. Signed averages can hide cancellation; these aggregate results do not establish feasibility for individual samples or measure inference speed.
